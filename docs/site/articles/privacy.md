# Privacy (KVKK)

An address that can be linked to a person is personal data under KVKK (Law 6698).

- The **library** stores and sends nothing; all processing is local.
- The **playground** parses in your browser (WebAssembly); the address never leaves the device.
- The **API** is stateless and never logs request bodies. Hosting it outside Türkiye is a cross-border transfer under KVKK
  Article 9, so for production data **self-host** the container inside your own infrastructure.
- Benchmark data contains no personal addresses: synthetic rows, published institution addresses and hand-written cases only.

This page is not legal advice.
